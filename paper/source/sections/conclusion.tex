\section{Conclusion and Data Availability}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper addresses the alert triage bottleneck in cloud DevSecOps pipelines by introducing an auditable final-byte evidence framework for edge firmware. Our results yield three primary conclusions:
First, observable final-byte syntactic evidence effectively distinguishes actionable command injections from benign or trapped parameters, validating 36 command-injection candidates with complete evidence without over-approximating reachability closures.
Second, content-bound audit records preserve the exact analysis scope, safely accounting for all 326 unresolved obligations as explicit residual types rather than emitting ungrounded negative claims.
Third, on a controlled benchmark evaluating evidence classification, \tool achieves perfect classification accuracy across sanitization boundaries. In future work, we plan to extend this evidence model across a broader edge firmware ecosystem and investigate automated synthesis of vendor wrapper specifications.

The complete artifact bundle---including the framework source code, benchmark suites, schema definitions, verification receipts, and Docker environments---is available at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/tsds-artifact-4BBE/}. Commercial binaries are referenced by SHA-256 digests.
