\section{Data Availability}
\label{sec:data-availability}

To support independent reproducibility, the complete \tool artifact bundle---including the open-source Python framework, the 24-case ground-truth benchmark, cryptographic ledger schemas, and Docker-based replay scripts---is available at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/tsds-artifact-4BBE/}. Restricted commercial firmware binaries are referenced by SHA-256 digests.
