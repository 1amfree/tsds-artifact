# Manuscript evidence binding

`manuscript_counts.tex` is generated by the accepted TSDS v20 release. The
macros in `experiment_values.tex` transcribe only values audited in
`experiment_reports/tsds_v20_release_20260718_r7_v8`. The macros in
`post_review_values.tex` refer to separately dated scope and calibration
receipts, including the 24-case original-program benchmark and the full-corpus
current-source multi-state campaign `r13`; they are not pooled into the frozen
V20 ledger. The `r13` values are bound by the downloaded campaign
configuration, campaign conservation audit, current multi-state audit, vector
decision audit, and evidence-contract audit. These files keep the paper source
free of hand-edited campaign counts.

The paper deliberately distinguishes sink-semantic evidence (`VECTOR_SAT`,
`MATRIX_UNSAT`, and `NO_MODELED_SOURCE`) from static source obligations,
static warning reductions, and residual obligations.
